What your student signed up for.

DECA is a co-curricular business, marketing, and finance program — think mock trial, but for business. Students compete in roleplay and exam-based events at District, State, and international conferences, building skills that show up on college applications and, honestly, in job interviews. Vista Ridge is one of the strongest chapters in the country, and here's what that means for your family this year.

Real preparation

Business plans, financial statements, marketing pitches — the kind of work that translates directly to college and career readiness.

A track record

505+ ICDC qualifiers and 158+ finalists since 2010 — your student is joining a chapter with a genuine history of results.

Real supervision

Every competition and trip is chaperoned by the advisor and trained parent volunteers — students are never on their own.

FAQ

Common questions.

How do I know if my student made the competitive team?

Competitive placements (the 100 members who compete at District) are announced by officers and the advisor after fall practice roleplays and exams. Students are notified directly, and it's also announced at a general chapter meeting — check the calendar for the date closest to District CDC.

What does travel to District, State, and ICDC actually involve?

District CDC is a single day, local, no overnight stay. State CDC (Houston) and ICDC (Anaheim) are multi-day trips with chaperoned hotel stays and group transportation — students do not travel independently, and a full itinerary goes home before departure.

How do I stay updated during the year?

Three places: the scrolling banner at the top of this site for urgent updates, the full calendar page for every date, and the chapter's social accounts linked in the footer below. When in doubt, email the advisor.

Does my student need business experience to join?

No. Most members join with zero business background. DECA teaches the content from the ground up — meetings, workshops, and officer mentorship are built for beginners as much as returning competitors.
