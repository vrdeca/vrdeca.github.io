FAQ

Before you sign up.

Do I need experience to join?

No experience needed — DECA teaches you everything from scratch. Roleplay, exams, and business skills are taught for first-timers just as much as returning competitors.

Do I need to be enrolled in a business class?

Yes. Active enrollment in a Vista Ridge business, marketing, or finance course is required to be a DECA member — that's a hard requirement, not a suggestion. Talk to your counselor if you need to add one.

What if I don't want to compete?

Competing is optional. Plenty of members contribute through the school store, event coordination, and service projects instead — see "Get more involved" below.

How much does it cost to join?

Dues are [$XX] and cover DECA Inc. membership, chapter materials, and a t-shirt. Payment plans and need-based waivers are available — just ask.

Get more involved

Membership is the floor, not the ceiling.

There's a real place in this chapter for every member, not just the ~100 who compete each year. Here's how the rest of us keep Vista Ridge DECA running — and build skills that matter just as much doing it.

Ongoing

School Store

Run point-of-sale, manage inventory, and help operate the chapter's student-run store — real retail experience, built into the school day.

Per event

Event Coordination

Help plan and run District CDC logistics, chapter fundraisers, and member socials. Great fit if competing isn't your thing but organizing is.

Flexible

Community Service Hours

Chapter service projects throughout the year count toward NHS and other honor-society hour requirements — ask an officer what's coming up.
