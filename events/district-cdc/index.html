About District CDC

District CDC is where Vista Ridge's competitive season begins. Members test their business, marketing, finance, and hospitality skills through roleplays, exams, and presentations against every other chapter in the district. Top performers in each event qualify to advance to the Texas DECA State Career Development Conference.
