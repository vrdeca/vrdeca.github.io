About State CDC

The Texas DECA State Career Development Conference brings together the top-performing students from every district in the state for a second round of competition, along with leadership sessions, workshops, and networking with thousands of DECA members. Top finishers in each competitive event earn a spot at the DECA International Career Development Conference.
