About ICDC

The DECA International Career Development Conference is the pinnacle of the competitive season — a multi-day gathering of tens of thousands of students from across the United States and several other countries. Alongside the final rounds of competition, ICDC features keynote speakers, a career fair, leadership academies, and an awards ceremony recognizing the world's top young business leaders.
