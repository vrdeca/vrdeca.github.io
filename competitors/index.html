Resource

What's a Performance Indicator?

Performance Indicators (PIs) are the specific business concepts DECA judges score you on in a roleplay — think of them as the rubric hiding inside the scenario. Knowing your event's PIs cold is the single highest-leverage thing you can do before a competition.

Online challenges

No live event? Still compete.

Live conferences aren't the only way to put DECA skills to the test — and they're a great way to build reps before Districts even if you didn't land a competitive slot this semester.

Simulation

Stock Market Game

A semester-long virtual portfolio simulation run by SIFMA — trade real market data with fake money and get ranked against schools nationwide. Strong prep for Finance and Business Finance events, and open to any member regardless of competitive status.

Simulation

Knowledge Matters Virtual Business

Run a simulated retail, restaurant, or personal finance operation online, making the same pricing, staffing, and marketing calls a real manager would. Directly reinforces Business Administration and Marketing PIs.

Why it matters

Built-in bench depth

Online challenges give every member — not just the 100 who compete live — a way to build real business reps, pad a resume, and stay sharp for next year's tryout cycle. Ask an officer how to get registered.
